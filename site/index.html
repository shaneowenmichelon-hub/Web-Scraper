03 / About

An operator who turns culture into companies.

I'm Shane Michelon — a founder and operator working at the intersection of events, marketing, and real estate for the college market. I started building where I studied, at Tulane University, and turned it into a group of companies that reach students across the country.

As Co-Owner & President of ZMM Events and Co-Founder of the Night School tour, I help produce some of the biggest weekends on the college calendar. I founded Collegiate Agency to give brands a direct line into campus culture, and I'm Founder & CEO of S.O.S Consultants, fixing how students find a place to live. The throughline: find where students already are, and build something worth showing up for.
